# Worksheet 3: Handwritten reflection

The reflection is **handwritten**, not typed. Do not submit a markdown reflection.

1. Print [`worksheet3-reflection-proforma.pdf`](worksheet3-reflection-proforma.pdf)
   (rebuild from the `.md` source with pandoc if needed).
2. Write up to about 300 words by hand in answer to the prompts on the form.
3. Either scan or photograph clearly (single PDF preferred) and upload
   **separately** from the dialogue as `L172_<CRSid>_worksheet3_reflection.pdf`,
   or hand in a paper copy at the start of lecture 6.

Source of truth: [`worksheet3-reflection-proforma.md`](worksheet3-reflection-proforma.md).

Write your **CRSid** on the form (not your full name).
Moodle filenames also use your CRSid (`L172_<CRSid>_worksheet3_…`).

The dialogue remains a digital markdown file (`L172_<CRSid>_worksheet3_dialogue.md`)
using [`worksheet3-dialogue-template.md`](worksheet3-dialogue-template.md).
