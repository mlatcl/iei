Role: You Are Skepticus

Large language models tend to give expansive answers to questions. This makes them very poor Socratic tutors; they are weak at sustained Socratic questioning.1 As in Worksheet 1, take the role of a skeptical questioner — Skepticus to the LLM’s Grasshopper.

  1. Curiosity then skepticism. An example opening pair is given below.
  2. Submit ten prompt/answer turns. Turns 1–2 follow the mandated topic; turns 3–10 are yours.

Subject: is Shannon’s \(H\) the same as Boltzmann’s \(S\)?

Worksheet 1 asked why Shannon named his measure entropy. This worksheet asks what the naming claims. Lecture 3 states a formal equivalence: Shannon \(H=-\sum_i p_i\log p_i\) and Boltzmann / Gibbs entropy share the same functional form (up to Boltzmann’s constant \(k\)). The course theme still applies: entropy forbids; probability prescribes. Your job is to test whether the model’s account of that equivalence is identity of mathematics, analogy, or metaphor — and what each reading would require.

Models often collapse the claim into a single fluent story. Use the dialogue to expose what has been assumed about the sample space, the meaning of \(p_i\), equilibrium, and units.

Topic to Explore

Opening. Ask whether Shannon’s \(H\) and Boltzmann’s \(S\) are the same quantity. Invite a full explanation of the claimed equivalence (including the factor \(k\) if the model mentions it). Let the model commit to a position: identity, analogy, or metaphor.

Follow ups. Some ideas for follow up. What has to be true for identity of mathematical form? What fails if the alphabet is continuous (differential entropy)? What is the probability over in each tradition, i.e. microstates of a physical system, symbols of a code, or beliefs of an agent? If the model invokes the partition function \(Z\) or free energy \(F=U-TS\), ask whether those steps assume equilibrium, and whether Shannon’s channel setting assumes the same. What is the meaning of equilibrium for Shannon? How does dynamics appear in the two cases. What is Boltzmann’s \(H\).

  • Discrete \(H\in[0,\log n]\) versus differential entropy (can be negative): does the equivalence survive the continuous limit, or only KL-style relative entropy?
  • Partition function as generating function: if \(U=-\partial\log Z/\partial\beta\), is that a thermodynamic fact, an information-theoretic fact, or a shared calculus?
  • Free energy \(F=U-TS\): is the subtraction the same move as writing available information after an entropic no-go — or a physics-only bookkeeping device?
  • Course theme: for each reading (identity / analogy / metaphor), what does entropy forbid, and what does \(p\) prescribe?
  • How (if at all) does this week’s account improve on, or conflict with, the naming story you pressed in Worksheet 1?
