Purely Entropic Good Regulator

Residual outcome uncertainty is disturbance entropy minus information the regulator has about the disturbance.

  • Disturbance \(D\), response \(R\), essential variable \(E\)
  • \(H(E)\ge H(D)-I(D;R)\)
  • Useless variety appears as small \(I(D;R)\)
Named tool for LO13, not a new outcome. Ashby’s requisite variety in Shannon form (Ashby, 1956). Quantity, not structure. Perfect regulation \(H(E)=0\) forces \(I(D;R)=H(D)\).

One shot, not an MDP: choose a policy \(\pi(a\mid s)\) to minimise outcome entropy.

  • State \(S\), action \(A\), outcome \(Z\sim p(z\mid s,a)\)
  • Objective: \(\min_\pi H(Z)\)
  • Model (weak): \(H(A\mid S)=0\), i.e. \(A=h(S)\)
LO13. Board this setup. Not Bellman, not reward. Entropy of \(Z\) is Conant and Ashby’s success criterion. Ten to twelve minutes for the proof block.

Randomising between outcome-distinct actions cannot minimise \(H(Z)\).

  • At state \(s\), mix \(a_1,a_2\) with \(\psi(s,a_1)=z_1\ne z_2=\psi(s,a_2)\)
  • Transfer mass \(\alpha\): \((u,v)\mapsto(u-\alpha,v+\alpha)\)
  • \(g''(\alpha)<0\): interior mixture is not a minimum
Board \(g(\alpha)=-(u-\alpha)\log(u-\alpha)-(v+\alpha)\log(v+\alpha)\). Strict concavity is the engine. Deterministic \(\psi\) for this lemma.

Among \(H(Z)\)-minimisers there exists a deterministic policy: \(H(A\mid S)=0\).

  • Pick one action per \(s\) among those sharing \(z^\ast(s)\)
  • \(A=h(S)\) leaves \(p(z)\), hence \(H(Z)\), unchanged
  • Stochastic channel: same existence via extreme points
Theorem: exists optimal \(\pi\) with \(H(A\mid S)=0\). Not every optimal policy is deterministic — only that a simplest one is. Course close remains: entropy forbids; probability prescribes.

The theorem is weaker than the popular slogan.

  • Weak model: constant \(A=a_0\) also has \(H(A\mid S)=0\)
  • Low \(H(Z)\) means predictable, not desirable
  • Catastrophe with \(H(Z)=0\) is ``optimal’’ under entropy alone
Do not collapse \(H(A\mid S)=0\) into ``\(A\) contains a rich model of \(S\).’’ Faithful models need \(H(S\mid A)=0\) as well. Desirability needs a goal set \(G\), not entropy alone.

IB gloss, not the theorem: keep only distinctions that matter for action.

  • \(\min I(S;A)\) subject to \(H(Z)\le\epsilon\) — course reading
  • Deterministic \(A=h(S)\) gives \(I(S;A)=H(A)\)
  • Do not attribute that optimisation to Conant and Ashby
IB was LO10. This gloss sits after the earned theorem. Course punchline remains Week 1: entropy forbids; probability prescribes.
