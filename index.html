--- layout: lecture-home title: Information, Entropy and Intelligence ---

Develop the mathematical connections between thermodynamics, information theory, and Bayesian inference, and understand how they provide a toolkit for reasoning about the foundations of intelligent systems.

L172, Michaelmas 2026. Eight Tuesdays, 13 October – 1 December, in FW26. ACS MPhil / Part III.

Entropy appears in three traditions — thermodynamics, information theory, and Bayesian inference — and is the same mathematical object under different operational assumptions. The split we use throughout is: entropy forbids, probability prescribes. The module builds the machinery needed to treat superintelligence claims the way the second law treats perpetual motion, and it takes the human–machine bandwidth gap of The Atomic Human as a second, human, no-go.
